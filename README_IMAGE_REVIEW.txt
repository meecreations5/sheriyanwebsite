SHANERYN — STOCK PHOTO REVIEW BUILD

Open index.html to choose a concept. Both designs retain their approved HTML layout and interactions.

High-resolution free-stock images are referenced from Pexels image URLs (generally 2400px wide). An internet connection is needed to load them. If loading fails, the original local prototype image appears automatically.

Photography is illustrative ONLY; it does not represent actual SHANERYN garments, clients, or endorsed models. Check the Pexels licence and the exact selected image, including restrictions on branding/model endorsement, before public commercial use.

IMAGE_SOURCE_REGISTER.csv maps each image slot to its online stock source and local fallback.

Photos selected from verified publicly accessible Pexels listing IDs. Direct photo-server delivery could not be tested from this environment, and may be unavailable in some browsers/networks. If it fails, the original image appears. This is not a fully offline HD-photo package.
